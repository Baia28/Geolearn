The current review engine implements a simplified SuperMemo (SM-2) Spaced Repetition System (SRS), but it is currently underutilizing the tags and metadata generated by your content importer.  How Current Review & SRS Logic Operates

Priority Queueing: For unit reviews, items are sorted by next_review_date ASC (overdue cards first) and ease_factor ASC (most difficult cards first). 
Global reviews target items where next_review_date <= today, prioritized by lowest ease_factor and lowest mastery_level.  

Backfill Strategy: If fewer than 12 cards are due for review, it fills remaining slots with unlearned unit items or unreviewed global items ordered by lowest mastery.  

Adaptive Intervals: Correct answers increase repetitions and bump ease_factor up by +0.1 (max 3.0), multiplying the interval up to 120 days. Incorrect answers reset repetitions and interval_days to 0 while dropping ease_factor by -0.2 (min 1.3).  


How I Can Elevate the SRS Logic To make the review engine significantly smarter using your imported metadata:

Latency-Weighted Difficulty: Your log records response_latency_ms. A correct answer that takes 8 seconds indicates high hesitation; we can lower its ease_factor even if answered correctly.  

Tag-Based Review Distractors: Pass importer tags to distractor generators during review mode so distractors belong to similar semantic groups (e.g., food words against food words).

Dynamic Importer Seeding: Set initial SRS ease_factor based on importer difficulty ratings instead of defaulting every word to 2.5.
